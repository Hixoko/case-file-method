# Gap analysis — Patient A

As of **M63**. The ordered panel leans inflammatory and rheumatological. Casting
wide is reasonable — but the tests most likely to explain *this* pattern (brisk arm
reflexes with absent ankle jerks) are mostly not on it.

## Ordered

| Test | Verdict |
|---|---|
| MRI cervico-dorsal spine | ✅ Exactly right. **Confirm contrast** |
| Vitamin B12 | ✅ Right test — ⚠ compromised by the supplement prescribed the same day |
| Vitamin D | ✅ Sensible — deficiency causes weakness and bone fragility |
| Creatine kinase | ◻ Reasonable, though EMG already found no myopathy |
| Antinuclear antibody panel | ◻ Connective-tissue screen |
| HLA-B27 | ◻ Spondyloarthropathy screen — sacroiliac joints reported normal |
| X-ray knees, pelvis and hips | ✅ Sensible after years of knee buckling and hip weakness |

## Missing — same needle, same visit

| Test | Why it matters for this pattern |
|---|---|
| **Methylmalonic acid + homocysteine** | What makes a B12 result trustworthy. B12 alone misses a meaningful share of true deficiency — and this one is already compromised |
| **Copper, ceruloplasmin, zinc** | Copper deficiency is the near-perfect mimic. Needs all three. Excess zinc causes it |
| **Intrinsic factor + parietal cell antibodies** | Diagnose pernicious anaemia — directly relevant given autoimmune thyroid disease |
| **Blood count with MCV, folate, vitamin E** | Cheap, same draw. Large red cells would strongly support H1 |
| **HbA1c / fasting glucose** | Diabetes is the commonest cause worldwide of absent ankle jerks with burning feet. Should not be assumed absent |

## Missing — independent of the neurology

| Investigation | Why |
|---|---|
| **Kidney ultrasound + creatinine** | Three months catheterised with no kidney check. Retained urine damages kidneys silently. Does not wait for a diagnosis |
| **Bladder / urodynamic assessment** | The consultation note itself records that the retention *"has not been formally evaluated"* — it failed at M60 |
| **Continence or urology review** | After three months, intermittent catheterisation may be safer than an indwelling tube |
| **Lying and standing blood pressure** | Free, five minutes. The M60 fall **began with dizziness** — the one orphan symptom in the causal graph |
| **Vibration sense, 128 Hz tuning fork** | Recorded nowhere. In B12 deficiency vibration goes **first**, before position sense. Under a minute |
| **Bone density scan** | Never done. Post-chemotherapy and now immobile |

## Care gaps — in nobody's notes because they are nobody's specialty

- **Pressure sores.** Prolonged sitting with reduced sensation. Daily skin checks
  over tailbone, hips and heels; a pressure-relieving cushion **before** the first
  sore, not after.
- **Venous thrombosis.** New immobility plus profound weakness. A calf that becomes
  swollen, warm or painful needs same-day attention.
- **Disuse atrophy.** The EMG says the muscle is intact and trainable, and the notes
  record that physiotherapy *reduced* the knee buckling. A wheelchair is a reason to
  change the physiotherapy plan, not to stop it.
- **The allergy is not on the prescription.** The allergies field is blank. With an
  indwelling catheter, the likeliest infection is reflexively treated with the drug
  class she reacts to.

---

**Note the shape of this list.** The highest-value items are not diagnostic
insights. They are a kidney check, a tuning fork, and a blood pressure cuff — things
that fall between specialties because each belongs to someone who is not in the
room.
