# Timeline — Patient A

Dates are months from the index event. **▼** marks a *step* — a sudden drop rather
than gradual decline.

| When | What happened | Where it left her |
|---|---|---|
| **M0** | Uterine malignancy — surgery, chemotherapy, pelvic radiotherapy | **▼** Weakness while walking first noticed *during* chemotherapy; attributed to treatment |
| **~M14** | Fall from a table, with back injury | **▼** Now needs one person's support to walk. First clear step down |
| **M14 → M55** | ~10 falls, mostly knees buckling | Stick, then walker. Physiotherapy **reduced** the buckling — a documented treatment response |
| **M60** | Dizziness, then a fall — **and the same day, the bladder stopped** | **▼** Catheter placed. Two failed removal attempts. Still catheterised |
| **M60 + 2 days** | MRI spine (screening, no contrast) | Clean — no fracture, no compression, cauda equina unremarkable, cord normal |
| **M62** | Nerve conduction study, EMG, SSEP | The cord is not **conducting** properly although it **looks** normal |
| **M62 → M63** | Walker to wheelchair in four weeks | **▼** From 25–30 steps with a walker to needing her legs lifted into the chair |
| **M63** | Neurology consultation | New history and examination findings that change the picture. Tests and medicines ordered. **Still no named diagnosis** |

## The reframe

Three of the four steps follow a fall. The natural reading, carried through five
years of referrals, was *she fell, and the fall damaged something.*

But the imaging **two days after** the M60 fall found no structural damage of any
kind.

So the other reading fits better: **the cord took a sudden step down, and that step
caused the fall.**

Conditions that decline in discrete steps are a shorter and more specific list than
conditions that decline gradually — and that shorter list contains treatable
entries. This single observation changed which specialty the case belonged to.
