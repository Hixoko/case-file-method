# Disclaimer

**This repository contains no medical advice, and nothing in it is a diagnosis.**

It describes techniques for organising and cross-reading documents you already
have. It is written by a family member, not a clinician. It has not been reviewed
by any medical professional, institution, or regulatory body.

## What this means in practice

- **Never start, stop, or change a medicine** on the basis of anything here or
  anything the method produces. That is a conversation with the treating clinician,
  every time, without exception.
- **The output of this method is a question, not a conclusion.** If you find
  yourself with a conclusion, you have gone too far. Technique 8 exists to convert
  findings back into questions.
- **A hypothesis that fits well is still a hypothesis.** The worked example's
  leading candidate fits ten independent findings and may still be wrong. The
  method tracks candidates with a *status*, never a verdict, for exactly this reason.
- **Your doctors know things this method cannot see** — examination findings,
  pattern recognition from thousands of patients, and the parts of the record you
  do not have.

## What the method is actually for

Making the twenty minutes you get with a specialist count: arriving with the whole
file read, the contradictions noted, and the three questions that matter, instead
of discovering on the drive home what you should have asked.

That is a real contribution and it is the limit of the claim being made.

## Worked example

The case in [`example/`](example/) is real and is published with the consent of the
family member who assembled it. It has been de-identified: no name, no clinicians,
no institutions, no locations, no absolute dates, no images. Clinical values are
retained because they carry the teaching.

Nothing in the example should be read as guidance for any other patient. Two
people with the same test results can have different conditions, and the example
exists to show **how the techniques were applied**, not what they concluded.
